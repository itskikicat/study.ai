  • Hebrew translation and right-to-left layout.

  • About Eduora — introduced a new section explaining that Eduora is a website for students.
  • Made in Czechia — added a Czech identity note and flag.

  • Partner logo — uses the exact Mind Garden image supplied by the user and links to its website.

  • New admin control center — account management, site settings, and service health.
